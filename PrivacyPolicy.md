## 1. Overview

PDF to Audiobook (the "App") is a Windows 11 desktop application that converts PDF, EPUB and plain-text (.txt) files into spoken audio saved as MP3 or WAV files. This policy explains what information the App handles and how. In short: **the App is designed to work on your computer, and we do not collect, store or sell your personal information.**

## 2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, transmit or have access to:

- The documents you open (PDF, EPUB, TXT) or their text
- The audio files (MP3, WAV) you create
- Your name, email address, location or other identifying details
- Usage analytics, telemetry, crash reports or advertising identifiers

The App does not require an account or sign-in.

## 3. How your files are processed

All text extraction and speech synthesis happens locally on your device. Your source documents and generated audio files stay on your computer, in the locations you choose. The App reads files only when you select them and writes audio files only where you tell it to save them. We cannot see, recover or delete these files.

## 4. Local settings

The App may store preferences on your device (for example, chosen voice, speaking rate, output format and folder, window size). This data stays on your computer and is removed when you uninstall the App or delete its settings folder.
